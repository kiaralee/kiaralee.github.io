Kiara Hosie

Oboe & English Horn

Hi, I'm Kiara Hosie (she/her). I'm an oboist of over 10 years based in Vancouver, BC. I teach private oboe lessons online and in-person. I am also available for in-school sectionals and clinics. Please email me with inquiries!

About

I completed my BMus with a major in musicology and sound studies and a minor in psychology at the University of Victoria in 2023. I had the pleasure of studying with Russell Bajer of the Victoria Symphony. I am currently working towards an MMus in oboe performance at the University of British Columbia under the tutelage of Geronimo Mendoza. When I am not appearing as a soloist, I am also an active performer in ensembles across the Greater Victoria and Vancouver areas. I am experienced with a range of styles including contemporary music, chamber music, musical theatre, symphony orchestra, and wind band.

When I'm not studying or in the practice room, you can find me hanging out with my cat, baking cookies, and reading about Greek mythology.

Oboe Lessons

As a private oboe teacher with over 4 years of experience, I take a patient and individualised approach. It's important to me to meet students where they're at, helping them set and meet their own musical goals to reach their full potential. Lessons with me focus not only on building technical skills and learning repertoire, but also developing aural and theory skills, healthy habits, strong practice routines. I welcome students of all ages and abilities— it's never too late to start! Contact me to start your oboe journey! First lessons always come with 1 complimentary handmade reed. I currently teach virtual and in-home lessons and offer a flexible schedule.

Handmade Reeds
